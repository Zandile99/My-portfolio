About Me 👩

Who Am I?

My name is Zandile Kama. I was born and raised in a small town called uMqanduli in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. I completed my secondary education at Manzelwandle Sandile Senior Secondary School and successfully obtained my matric certificate in 2018.

In 2019, I enrolled at Walter Sisulu University to study Information Technology. Through dedication and hard work, I completed my qualification and graduated in 2022. I am currently studying towards an Advanced Diploma in ICT in Application Development at Walter Sisulu University.

I began my professional journey as a Software Developer Intern at PAICTA, where I gained practical experience in software development and programming. I later Department of Health Frere Hospital as an HMS2 Intern, where I worked as a System Administrator, Desktop Support Technician, HMS Facilitator, and IT Technician. My responsibilities included user account setup and support, hardware and software maintenance, troubleshooting technical issues, and supporting healthcare information systems.

Currently, I am a Systems Analyst Student at Mercedes-Benz South Africa. This opportunity is helping me develop my analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills while gaining valuable industry experience in a professional environment.

My Career Goals

My goal is to continue growing my technical skills and expand my knowledge in systems analysis, software development, and information technology. I aspire to build a successful career in the technology industry and contribute to innovative solutions that improve business processes and people's lives.
